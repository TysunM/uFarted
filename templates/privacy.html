Privacy Policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

{{APP}} is free and built to collect as little as possible. We don’t ask for your email, phone number or real name, we don’t run ads, and we don’t sell or share your data. If that ever changes (for example, if we add ads), we’ll update this policy and tell you in the app first.

What we store

  • Account: an account ID, and — if you claim one — a username plus a scrambled (hashed) password and recovery code. We can’t read either.
  • Your logs: what you record in the app: fart/burp type, time, smell, discomfort, trigger tags, private notes and daily gut checks (bloating and belly pain). These are visible only to you.
  • Recordings: clips you choose to record. They’re private unless you turn on “Share to the community”.
  • Community activity: posts you share, reactions, reports and blocks.
  • Technical data: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and standard server logs (IP address, timestamps, requested pages) used for security and abuse prevention and rotated automatically.

Health information

Your logs may be considered health-related information. We use them only to show you your own stats, insights and reports. They are never public, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties except our hosting provider, which stores them on our behalf.

What’s public

Only clips you explicitly share: the recording, its type, when it was posted, your username and reactions. Your notes, smell ratings, triggers, discomfort and counts are never shared.

Your choices

  • Export: download all your logs as CSV any time from the Me tab.
  • Delete: delete any log, clip or post individually, or delete your entire account from the Me tab. Deletion is immediate; copies in our rolling backups expire within 7 days.
  • Guest data: if you never claim a username and your session ends, your data is deleted automatically.

Cookies

We use one strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in. No tracking or advertising cookies.

Children

Accounts are for people 13 and older. Before creating an account we ask for your birth year (and month, if needed) only to check your age. We don’t store that answer. Younger visitors can listen to the public community feed without an account, and we don’t collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to processing. Most of this you can do yourself in the app; for anything else, contact us.
